## 2026-06-11 - Non-blocking Redirect UI
**Learning:** Native `alert()` calls are jarring and break the immersion of a modern web application. Replacing them with a themed countdown component provides better UX, maintains the app's visual identity, and gives users agency with a manual override.
**Action:** Always prefer custom, non-blocking UI notifications or countdowns over native browser dialogs like `alert()` or `confirm()`.
