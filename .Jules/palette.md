## 2025-05-14 - Interactive Elements Visibility and Accessibility
**Learning:** In a dashboard with many card-based links, secondary actions like "Add to Favorite" are often hidden on hover for visual cleanliness. This makes them inaccessible to keyboard users unless they are also shown on focus-visible. Additionally, icon-only buttons require explicit ARIA labels and pressed states for screen readers. Native browser alerts for redirects are jarring and provide poor UX compared to themed, accessible countdown interstitials.
**Action:** Always include `focus-visible:opacity-100` alongside `group-hover:opacity-100` for hidden actions. Use `aria-label` and `aria-pressed` for toggleable icon buttons. Replace `alert()` with a `role="status"` interstitial with a countdown and manual override.
